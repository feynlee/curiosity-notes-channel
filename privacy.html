Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Curiosity Notes is an educational video channel run by one person. This page covers the Curiosity Notes Publisher tool and this website.

What the tool stores

When the channel owner connects TikTok, the tool stores the TikTok open ID, display name, avatar link and the access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues. Tokens are kept on the owner's own computer, in its secure keychain. They are not sent to any other service.

What the tool does with it

It uses that access only to post the channel's own videos to the owner's account and, optionally, to read the owner's own video list and view counts. It does not read private messages, follow accounts, or post anything the owner has not approved.

Visitors to this website

This site has no accounts, no cookies we set and no analytics. Our host may keep ordinary server logs.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. We do not use TikTok data for advertising or to train AI models.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, and can revoke access in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions. To ask about data, write to curiositynotes@gmail.com.
