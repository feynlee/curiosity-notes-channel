Curiosity Notes Publisher

This tool sends finished Curiosity Notes videos to TikTok. The channel owner signs in with TikTok once, and TikTok returns to this site to finish the sign-in.

The tool sends each video as a draft. The owner then reviews it in the TikTok app and chooses whether to post it. Every video is original, and we label it as AI-generated.
